# Direction artistique — version 0.2

Images créées avec le générateur d’images intégré, puis intégrées à Unity comme décor et atlas de sprites avec transparence. Les dimensions effectivement obtenues peuvent différer des dimensions demandées. Les poses sont découpées en cellules dans Unity, sans retouche des images sources.

Fichiers : `Assets/Resources/Art/paris.png`, `heroes.png`, `enemies.png`.

## Prompts finaux

### background

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production panoramic background painting for a side-scrolling Unity 2D action game set in Paris in 2026. Premium hand-painted 2D arcade game art with finely controlled pixel-art clusters, 1990s run-and-gun animation craftsmanship with modern rich detail, expressive chunky silhouettes, strong dark navy outlines, warm ochre highlights, teal shadow palette, whimsical action-comedy mood. Actual production assets, not a screenshot, not mockup, not simplistic vector shapes. Create a very wide landscape panorama, approximately 3:1 aspect, 3072x1024 if possible. Orthographic SIDE VIEW of a continuous Paris boulevard, camera at character height, no perspective vanishing point along the playable path. Beautiful ornate Haussmann façades, zinc mansard roofs, wrought iron balconies, a corner café with red striped awning, bakery, weathered metro entrance, newspaper kiosk, antique street lamps, scattered crates and damaged shop shutters suggesting recent looting. Eiffel Tower visible above distant roofs, wisps of smoke in distant sky. Golden late afternoon with blue-green atmospheric skyline, cream stone, terracotta reds, warm glowing shop windows and detailed painterly textures. The walkable sidewalk baseline must be a perfectly horizontal line at exactly 80 percent from the TOP of the image; below this a clear 20 percent strip of asphalt and cobblestone foreground. Keep the playable strip open, no large objects occluding it. Ground-level props belong behind that baseline. Every building and landmark should be richly illustrated, crisp, and full of charming environmental storytelling. NO PEOPLE, NO characters, NO UI, NO HUD, NO watermark. Left and right edges have compatible building colors so adjacent repeated copies can meet unobtrusively. Deliver one finished full-bleed background image.

For EACH ROW the four columns are exactly: 1) idle aiming right; 2) running stride right with left leg forward; 3) running stride right with right leg forward; 4) airborne jump pose aiming right, knees tucked. Keep each hero face, outfit, weapon and proportions CONSISTENT across all four frames of that row. Exaggerated handsome action-comedy faces, beautifully shaded cloth, small gear details, readable silhouette. No muzzle flashes. Authentic high-end arcade sprite art, not geometric blocks. Real transparent alpha outside the figures.
